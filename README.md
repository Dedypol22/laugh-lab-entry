# The Robot Vacuum's Sock Alibi

A deadpan one-line excuse for the One-Liner Laugh Lab: the robot vacuum doesn't apologise for eating a sock, it cites the law — under vacuum law, anything left on the floor is lunch, and the sock was on the floor.

> "Under vacuum law, anything on the floor is lunch. Your sock was on the floor."
